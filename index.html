How it works

Everything, in one scroll.

01 — Log

Scan a meal with your camera.

Point at the plate and Deficit.today reads it. No searching a database, no scales, no barcodes — just a photo.

02 — Estimate

Calories and macros, before you save.

AI breaks the dish into ingredients with a calorie range and full macros. Adjust anything, then save it to your day.

03 — Read the day

Deficit or surplus, at a glance.

One big number and the mascot's face tell you where you stand — eaten vs. burned, with protein, carbs, fat and fiber underneath.

04 — Day & night

Dark mode, naturally.

The same clean layout, dialed for late-night logging. It follows your system, so it's just there when you need it.

05 — Home screen

Widgets keep the number close.

Today's deficit, eaten and burned — right on your home and lock screen. Check in without opening the app.

06 — Keep it up

Streaks and history that add up.

Your average deficit, day streak, and a week of bars — plus macro history — so a good day becomes a good month.
